# Course introduction videos

The source material for the videos in `static/course-intros/`. Each video is made in ElevenLabs: the lead
instructor's line spoken by an ElevenLabs voice, and the video made from the instructors' photos and that voice
with Seedance. The voice track is then put back in at full quality, with a still and captions made from the video.

`photos/` holds companion photos with a caption or name badge cropped off, since the video model copies what it
sees. The other companions' photos are used as they are, from their addresses in `app/starter_companions.csv`.
