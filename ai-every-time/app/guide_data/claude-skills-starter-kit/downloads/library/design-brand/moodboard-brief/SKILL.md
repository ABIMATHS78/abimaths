# Moodboard brief

Turn "something fresh but premium" into a direction everyone can see and agree on before the real work starts.

## Before you start

Ask for: the project and its audience, the feeling it should create, the words the team uses to describe it, examples they love and hate, and fixed elements such as brand colours. Ask in one short message.

## Steps

1. **Pin down the feeling.** Turn vague words into three to five precise ones, and say what each one doesn't mean, such as "calm, not sleepy".
2. **Suggest a colour direction**: a main colour, supporting colours and an accent, with hex values to start from and the mood each one gives. Check that text colours have enough contrast.
3. **Suggest a type direction**: the kind of typeface for headings and for text, such as a high-contrast serif or a rounded sans-serif, with a reason. Suggest freely licensed options where the budget is small.
4. **Describe the imagery**: subjects, light, colour treatment, framing, people and settings, and illustration or texture where relevant.
5. **Write search terms** for each part of the board, for stock libraries, photo sites or the team's own archive.
6. **Say what to avoid**: the clichés of the category, and styles that would send the wrong message.
7. **Offer two or three directions** if the brief is open, each with a name and a summary, so the team can choose.

## Output

```
# Moodboard brief: <project>
Feeling: <3 to 5 words, each with what it isn't>

## Direction: <name>
Colours: <hex values and roles>
Type: <heading and text styles, and why>
Imagery: <subjects, light, treatment>
Textures and details: <list>
Search terms: <list>
Avoid: <list>
```

## Rules

- References are for direction. Don't copy another brand's identity, or use images in the final work without a licence.
- Don't name living artists as styles to imitate. Describe the qualities instead, such as "soft natural light, muted greens".
- Keep the brand's fixed elements, and flag directions that conflict with them.
- Show people with care: represent the real audience in its variety, without stereotypes.

## Checklist before you finish

- [ ] The feeling is described precisely, with its limits.
- [ ] Colours have hex values and roles, and text contrast was checked.
- [ ] Each part of the board has search terms.
- [ ] What to avoid is listed.
