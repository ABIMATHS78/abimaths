# Brand guidelines check

Catch the off-brand details before they go out, with the rule for each one, so the fix is quick.

## Before you start

You need the brand guidelines, and the work to check: an image, a PDF, a page, slides or copy. Ask where the work will appear, and whether any exceptions were agreed. Ask in one short message. If there are no guidelines, say so, and offer to describe the brand's patterns from its existing work instead.

## Steps

1. **List the checkable rules** in the guidelines: logo versions, clear space, minimum size and placement; the colour palette and its values; typefaces, weights and sizes; the style of imagery and illustration; icons; the voice and spelling; and required legal text.
2. **Check the logo**: the right version for the background, enough clear space, a large enough size, and no stretching, recolouring, rotating or added effects.
3. **Check colours**: compare the values with the palette, and check the contrast of text for accessibility.
4. **Check type**: the right typefaces and weights, a clear hierarchy, and comfortable line spacing.
5. **Check imagery**: the style, subjects and treatment, and that each image has a licence or permission.
6. **Check the copy**: the voice, spelling, product names, claims and legal lines.
7. **Rank the issues**: must fix before release, should fix, and could improve.

## Output

```
| # | Issue | Where | Rule (quoted) | Priority | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

On brand: <what already follows the guidelines>
Couldn't check: <what the material didn't show, such as exact colour values in a screenshot>
```

## Rules

- **Check against the brand's own guidelines only.** Personal taste isn't a rule; offer it separately, labelled as a suggestion.
- Never guess a colour value from a compressed image. Say it needs checking in the source file.
- Quote the rule behind every issue.
- Flag any image, font or music without a known licence.

## Checklist before you finish

- [ ] Every issue quotes the rule it breaks.
- [ ] The issues are ranked.
- [ ] What couldn't be checked is listed.
- [ ] Licences for images and fonts were considered.
