# Brand voice guide

Help everyone who writes for a brand sound like the same brand, whether they're writing ads, emails or support replies.

## Before you start

Ask for: examples of writing the team thinks sounds right, and some that doesn't; the brand's values or positioning; the audience; and the places the voice will be used. Ask in one short message.

## Steps

1. **Study the good examples**: sentence length, formality, humour, the words used, and how readers are addressed.
2. **Name three or four voice traits**, each as "this, not that", such as "confident, not arrogant" or "warm, not sugary", and explain each in a sentence.
3. **Show how the tone shifts by situation.** The voice stays the same, but the tone changes. Cover the situations that matter: launches, help and support, apologies and bad news, sales, and social media.
4. **Make a word list**: words and phrases to use, words to avoid, and how to write the brand's name and its product names.
5. **Set the mechanics**: the spelling variant, capitals in headings, contractions, emoji, exclamation marks, and how to address the reader.
6. **Write before-and-after rewrites**, at least three, using the brand's own real copy.
7. **Add a quick test**: four or five questions a writer can ask before publishing.

## Output

```
# <Brand> voice guide
## Who we are, in one paragraph
## Our voice
| Trait | This | Not that | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
## Tone by situation
## Words we use and avoid
## Mechanics
## Before and after
## A quick test before you publish
```

## Rules

- Base the guide on the brand's own writing and values. Don't invent a personality the brand doesn't have, or claims it can't back up.
- Keep it inclusive: avoid words that exclude or stereotype people. Plain language helps everyone.
- Don't copy another company's voice guide or slogans.
- Keep the guide short enough to use. Long rules get ignored.

## Checklist before you finish

- [ ] Each trait has a "not that", to show its limits.
- [ ] The tone is shown for the situations that matter, including bad news.
- [ ] The rewrites use the brand's real copy.
- [ ] There's a quick test for writers.
