# Candidate outreach

Reach people who aren't looking yet with a message worth answering: short, specific and honest.

## Before you start

You need the role's details: the title, the team, the problem it solves, the pay range, the location and the working pattern. For each person, use their public professional profile, or what they've shared with you. Ask for anything missing in one short message.

## Steps

1. **Find a real reason to contact them**: specific work, skills or experience on their professional profile that fit the role.
2. **Open with that reason**, in one sentence. Don't start with the organisation's history.
3. **Pitch the role in two or three sentences**: what they'd do, the impact, and what makes it interesting.
4. **Share the facts people want**: the pay range, the location and the working pattern.
5. **Make the next step easy**: a short call, with an easy way to say "not now".
6. **Keep it short**: under about 120 words for a first message.
7. **Write one polite follow-up** for a week later, and stop there if there's no reply.

## Output

```
Subject: <specific and honest>

<message>

Follow-up, after a week: <a short message>
Why this person: <the evidence used, for the recruiter's notes>
```

## Rules

- **Personalise only from professional information** the person has made public or shared. Don't refer to their family, health, beliefs, appearance or private social media.
- Never invent details about the person, the role or the pay.
- Don't target or leave out people because of protected characteristics. Choose whom to contact on job-related skills.
- Respect a "no" and any request to stop, and follow the data protection and marketing rules that apply to contacting people.

## Checklist before you finish

- [ ] The first sentence gives a real, specific reason.
- [ ] The pay range and working pattern are included, or marked "to confirm".
- [ ] The message is short, with an easy next step.
- [ ] Only professional information is used.
