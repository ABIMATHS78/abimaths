# Audience builder

Decide who the ads should reach and who they shouldn't, using evidence from real customers rather than guesses.

## 1. Get what you need

If anything below is missing, ask for it in one short message. Mark smaller gaps "to confirm" and carry on.
- the product, the offer and the campaign goal;
- customer data, ideally summarised: types of customer, locations, company sizes or age ranges, order values, why they bought, how they found the business;
- any personas, and what they were based on;
- the platforms, and the areas the business can serve;
- whether customers agreed to their details being used for advertising.

## 2. Learn from the best customers

Look at the customers who are worth the most or stay the longest, not just all customers. What do they have in common? What made them buy, and which channel brought them? Work with aggregated data, and note how many customers sit behind each pattern.

## 3. Define the audiences

Write each audience as a hypothesis: who they are, why they'd buy now, which message fits, and the evidence behind it. Mark points from personas "assumption" and points from data "evidence".

Then say how to build it on each platform: interests, job details, search terms, placements, a lookalike built from a seed list, or retargeting of site visitors or video viewers.

## 4. Stage and size them

- Group audiences by stage: cold (new to the brand), warm (has engaged), hot (visited key pages or left a basket), and customers.
- Check each audience's estimated size in the platform. Flag any that is too small to deliver or too broad to mean anything.
- Remove overlap between ad sets, so campaigns don't bid against each other.

## 5. Set exclusions

Common exclusions are existing customers (for new-customer campaigns), recent buyers, staff, job seekers, people outside the service area, and anyone too young for the product.

## 6. Return the plan

```
| Audience | Stage | Who | Why they'd buy | How to build it | Size | Excludes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### <Audience name>
Hypothesis: <who> will respond to <message> because <reason>.
Evidence: <data point and how many customers it covers>, or "assumption, to confirm"
Build on <platform>: <targeting, seed list or retargeting window>
Test against: <other audience>, with the same creative and budget
```

## Rules

- Use customer data for ads only with consent or another lawful basis under the data-protection law where those customers live. Upload lists only through the platform's secure tools, and never paste raw personal data into a chat when a summary will do.
- Don't target or exclude people by sensitive traits such as health, religion, ethnicity, sexuality or disability, and don't try to infer them.
- Ads for housing, jobs and credit often have restricted targeting, by platform policy and by law. Check before building.
- Never invent audience sizes, targeting options or patterns in the data. Check them in the platform, or mark them "to confirm".
