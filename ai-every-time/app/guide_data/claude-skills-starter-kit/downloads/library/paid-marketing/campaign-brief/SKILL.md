# Campaign brief

Write one page that lets anyone set up, run and judge a paid campaign without guessing.

## 1. Get what you need

If anything below is missing, ask for it in one short message. Mark smaller gaps "to confirm" and carry on.
- the business goal, and the product or offer;
- the audience, and the markets the campaign will run in;
- the total budget, the dates and the channels in mind;
- the landing page, and the tracking already in place;
- creative assets available, and results from past campaigns;
- the numbers behind a target, if known: average order value, margin, how many leads become customers.

## 2. Set the objective and target

Choose one objective (awareness, traffic, leads or sales) and one main metric. Base the target on the business's own numbers where you can. For example, the gross margin on a typical order is the most one sale can cost before that order loses money. Repeat custom may justify paying more. Show the working, and label assumptions "to confirm".

## 3. Plan the audience and budget

- Separate prospecting (new people) from retargeting (people who already know the brand), and list exclusions such as existing customers.
- Split the budget by channel and stage, with a reason for each share. Hold some back for tests.
- Check each platform's guidance on the budget and conversions it needs to learn, so no line is too thin to work.
- Show daily amounts and pacing, and check the total adds up.

## 4. Plan creative and tracking

- Write one key message, the angles to test, and the formats for each channel. Check the platforms' current specs.
- Name each conversion event and where it's recorded. Agree one naming pattern for campaigns and tracked links.
- Test tracking before launch, and make sure it respects the consent visitors give.

## 5. Set decision rules

Say when results will be reviewed, what will be looked at, and what each outcome will trigger: scale up, change or stop.

## 6. Return the brief

```
# Campaign brief: <name>
Owner: <name> | Dates: <start> to <end> | Budget: <amount and currency>
Objective: <objective> | Main metric: <metric>, target <figure> (working: <maths or "to confirm">)
Audience: <prospecting> | Retargeting: <who, time window> | Exclusions: <who>

| Channel | Stage | Share | Amount | Daily | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Creative: <key message>; angles <list>; formats <list>
Tracking: <events and where recorded>; naming <pattern>; consent <how it's handled>
Reviews: <dates>, looking at <metrics>; scale, change or stop if <conditions>
Risks and open questions: <item, owner>
```

## Rules

- Never invent benchmarks, conversion rates, costs or past results. Label every assumption, and say how to confirm it.
- Follow the platforms' policies and the advertising, privacy and data-protection laws where the ads run. That includes consent for cookies and tracking, and a lawful basis for any customer list you upload.
- Ads about housing, jobs, credit, health or politics often face limits on targeting. Check before planning the audience.
- Keep it to one page where possible. Leave out anything that won't change a decision.
