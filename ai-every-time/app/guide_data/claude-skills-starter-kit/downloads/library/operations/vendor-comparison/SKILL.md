# Vendor comparison

Choose a vendor on the evidence, with criteria agreed before anyone falls for a demo.

## Ask first

If any of this is missing, ask for it in one short message:
- what's being bought, and the problem it must solve;
- the shortlisted vendors, and the information gathered so far: proposals, prices, demos, references;
- the budget, the timeline and any must-haves, such as security, data location or integrations;
- who decides.

## Steps

1. **Agree the criteria and weights first**, before scoring. Typical criteria: fit to requirements, total cost over the contract, quality, support, security and data protection, the vendor's stability, contract terms, and ease of switching later.
2. **Separate must-haves from scored criteria.** A vendor that fails a must-have is out, however well it scores elsewhere.
3. **Score each vendor** on each criterion from 1 to 5, with the evidence for each score.
4. **Calculate the weighted totals**, and show the working.
5. **Test the result**: would a small change in weights change the winner? If so, say so.
6. **Recommend**, with the main risks of the chosen vendor and how to reduce them in the contract or the rollout.

## Output

```
## Must-haves
| Must-have | Vendor A | Vendor B | Vendor C |
|---|---|---|---|

## Scores
| Criterion | Weight | Vendor A | Vendor B | Vendor C |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weighted total | 100% | | | |

## Recommendation
## Risks and how to manage them
## Gaps in the information
```

## Rules

- **Score only on evidence**: proposals, demos, references, documents. Never invent prices, features or certifications; mark unknowns "to confirm".
- Compare total cost, not just the headline price: set-up, licences, support, training and exit.
- Declare any conflict of interest, and keep the decision with the named decision-maker.
- Keep vendor proposals confidential.
