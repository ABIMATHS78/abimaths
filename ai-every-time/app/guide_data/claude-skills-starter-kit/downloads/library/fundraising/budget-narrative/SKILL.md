# Budget narrative

Explain every line of a grant budget, so an assessor can see what it buys, how it was worked out and why the project needs it.

## 1. Get what you need

If anything below is missing, ask for it in one short message. Mark smaller gaps "to confirm" and carry on.
- the budget, with line items, amounts and calculations;
- the project plan: activities, staff and timeline;
- the funder's rules on eligible costs, overheads and match funding, and any format or word limits;
- the basis for costs: quotes, salary scales, rates, past spending;
- contributions in kind, such as donated space or volunteer time.

## 2. Check the budget first

- Do the lines and totals add up?
- Does every line link to an activity in the application?
- Are all costs eligible under the funder's rules, and are overheads within their limit?
- Do staff time percentages match the roles described elsewhere in the application?
- Are inflation, currency and sales taxes, including whether tax can be reclaimed, handled clearly?

Flag every problem before writing. Don't explain away a figure that's wrong.

## 3. Justify each line

For each item, give what it is, the calculation (unit cost × quantity × time), the basis for the unit cost (a quote, salary scale, past spend or published rate), why the project needs it, and who pays: this funder, another source, or in kind.

## 4. Group and explain

Use the funder's own categories if they have them. Otherwise use staff, other staff costs such as employer contributions, travel, equipment, supplies, contracted services, other costs and overheads. Show how overheads were calculated, as the rate times the base the funder allows.

## 5. Return the narrative

```
## Budget summary
| Category | Requested | Other sources | Total |
|---|---|---|---|

### <Category>
<Line item>: <amount>
Calculation: <unit cost> × <quantity> × <time> = <total>
Basis: <source of the unit cost, with its date, or "to confirm">
Why it's needed: <the activity or outcome it supports>

## Overheads
<rate> × <base> = <amount>, as allowed by <funder rule>

## Match funding and in kind
| Source | Amount | Confirmed or pending |
|---|---|---|

## Issues to check
- <issue>
```

## Rules

- Never invent costs, quotes, salaries, rates or match funding. Use the budget given, and mark gaps "to confirm".
- Every figure in the narrative must match the budget exactly. Recalculate each one.
- Flag costs that may be ineligible. Never hide them or relabel them.
- Label estimates as estimates, and say what they're based on.
- Describe staff costs by role, and don't reveal an individual's pay without their consent, in line with the data-protection rules that apply.
