# Call follow-up

Send a follow-up the buyer could forward to colleagues, and leave CRM notes good enough for anyone to pick up the deal.

## 1. Get what you need

If anything below is missing, ask for it in one short message. Mark smaller gaps "to confirm" and carry on.
- the call notes or transcript, and who attended, with roles;
- what was agreed, and anything either side promised;
- the CRM fields the team fills in, such as stage, amount, close date and next step;
- the sender's usual tone.

## 2. Pull out the facts

From the notes, find:
- their situation and priorities, in their own words;
- the problems and their impact, with any figures they gave;
- who decides, how, and by when;
- budget signals, concerns, and competitors or alternatives mentioned;
- what each side promised, and when you'll next speak.

## 3. Write the email

- Thank them in one line, then sum up what you heard in three to five points, in their words, and invite corrections.
- List the next steps, each with an owner and a date, and attach or link anything you promised.
- End with one clear next action.
- Keep it short enough to read on a phone, and fit to forward: no jargon and no internal opinions.

## 4. Write the CRM notes

Record facts, not impressions. Where you add a judgement, label it as one. Give the reason for the stage, so a colleague can check it.

## 5. Return both

```
## Follow-up email
Subject: <subject>
<body>

## CRM notes
Account: <company>
Contacts: <name, role, part in the decision>
Stage: <stage>, because <evidence from the call>
Problem and impact: <their words, with any figures they gave>
Decision process: <who, how, when>
Budget: <what was said, or "not discussed">
Timeline: <dates>
Competitors or alternatives: <list>
Risks: <list>
Next step: <action>, <owner>, <date>

## Gaps to cover next time
- <gap>
```

## Rules

- Never invent commitments, figures, dates or quotes. If the notes don't say, write "to confirm" and list it for the seller.
- Don't overstate the stage, the deal size or how keen the buyer is.
- Record only what the business relationship needs. Leave out personal details shared in passing, and follow the data-protection laws where the contact lives.
- If the notes come from a recording, check that everyone agreed to be recorded.
- The email is a draft for the seller to check and send.
