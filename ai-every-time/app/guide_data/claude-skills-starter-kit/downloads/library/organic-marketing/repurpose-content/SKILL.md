# Repurpose content

Get more from one strong piece by turning it into posts that each work on their own channel, without changing what the source says.

## 1. Get what you need

If anything below is missing, ask for it in one short message. Mark smaller gaps "to confirm" and carry on.
- the source: full text, or a transcript with timestamps for audio and video;
- the channels, and the audience on each;
- the goal: visits to the original, followers, sign-ups or something else;
- how many posts they want, and over what period;
- brand rules: tone, words to avoid, accounts to tag.

## 2. Mine the source

Read or listen to all of it before writing anything. Pull out:
- the core message, in one sentence;
- five to ten ideas that each make sense on their own;
- lines worth quoting, and figures with the source the original gives, with timestamps for audio or video;
- stories, examples, practical tips, and any surprising point.

## 3. Match ideas to channels

Pick the idea and format that suit each channel's habits:
- professional networks: a lesson or opinion with context, in short paragraphs;
- short-text platforms: one sharp point, or a thread that unpacks a list;
- image-led platforms: a carousel of steps, or a quote card with a caption;
- short video: a script with a hook in the first seconds and on-screen text;
- newsletter or blog: a summary, or a short piece on one sub-topic, linking to the original.

Check each platform's current length and format limits, or ask. Don't rely on remembered numbers.

## 4. Write each post

- One idea per post, with an opening line that works for someone who never saw the original.
- Rewrite for each channel. Never paste the same text everywhere.
- Keep every fact, figure and quote exactly as in the source.
- Link back to the original where it serves the goal, and spread the posts over a few weeks.

## 5. Return the set

```
Core message: <one sentence>

### <Channel>: <idea>
<post, ready to paste>
Visual: <what to show, or "none"> | From: <section or timestamp> | Length: <count>, checked against the current limit

Clips, for audio or video: <start and end> | <what happens> | <why it works> | <caption>
Schedule: <day> | <channel> | <post>
```

## Rules

- Never add facts, figures, quotes or claims that aren't in the source. If a post needs one, mark it "to confirm".
- Keep quotes in context, so no one appears to say something they didn't mean.
- Credit guests and co-authors by name, and check they're happy to be quoted or clipped.
- Repurpose only content the person owns or has the rights to use.
- Label sponsored content and affiliate links as the advertising rules where the audience lives require, and respect the privacy of anyone shown or named.
