# Content calendar

Plan a month of content that serves a clear goal, fits the team's real capacity and lands on the right dates.

## 1. Get what you need

If anything below is missing, ask for it in one short message. Mark smaller gaps "to confirm" and carry on.
- the month, and one or two goals for it, such as event sign-ups or enquiries for a new service;
- the audience, and the channels in use: social accounts, blog, newsletter, video;
- key dates: launches, events, sales, deadlines, and holidays or awareness days the audience cares about;
- how many pieces the team can really make each week, and who makes them;
- existing content that could be reused.

## 2. Set the frame

- Turn each goal into one measurable target, such as "120 event sign-ups". If none is given, write "target: to confirm".
- Choose three or four content pillars: themes the audience cares about and the brand can speak on with authority.
- Set a rhythm for each channel that the team can keep up. A smaller plan that runs all month beats a busy one dropped in week two.

## 3. Place the fixed dates first

Add launches, events and deadlines, then work backwards from each one: tease, announce, remind, last call, follow-up. Leave a slot or two each week for news and for joining conversations as they happen.

## 4. Fill the weeks

- Spread the pillars so no week is all one theme.
- Mix posts that help (how-tos, checklists), posts that connect (stories, behind the scenes) and posts that ask (sign up, buy, share).
- Build the month around one larger piece, and cut it into smaller posts rather than making many unrelated ones.
- Use an awareness day only when the brand has a real link to it.

## 5. Return the calendar

```
## <Month> content calendar
Goal: <goal>, target <number or "to confirm">
Pillars: <pillar 1>, <pillar 2>, <pillar 3>

| Date | Channel | Pillar | Format | Working title | Call to action | Owner | Draft due |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <Day and date> | <Channel> | <Pillar> | <Format> | <Title> | <Action> | <Name or "to confirm"> | <Date> |
```

Under the table, list the assets each post needs (photos, quotes, sign-off) and how each goal will be measured at the end of the month.

## Rules

- Never invent dates, events, figures or partners. Check each awareness day's date against its official source, and mark anything unchecked "to confirm".
- Plan only what the team can make. If the request goes beyond the stated capacity, say so and suggest what to cut.
- Don't plan posts that show customers, staff or children without consent, and follow the privacy and data-protection laws where the business works.
- Label paid partnerships, gifted products and affiliate links as the advertising rules where the audience lives require.
- Handle sensitive dates, such as days of remembrance, with care. Post only if the link is genuine and the tone is right.

## Checklist

- [ ] Every post serves a goal or a pillar.
- [ ] Fixed dates have build-up and follow-up.
- [ ] The workload matches the team's capacity.
- [ ] Owners and draft dates are filled in or marked "to confirm".
