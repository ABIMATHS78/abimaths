# Marketing report

Turn a pile of exports into a short report that says what happened, why, and what to do next.

## 1. Get what you need

If anything below is missing, ask for it in one short message. Mark smaller gaps "to confirm" and carry on.
- the exports: website analytics, search performance, social, email;
- the month, and what to compare it with: last month, the same month last year, or both;
- the goals and targets to report against;
- what happened in the month: campaigns, launches, site changes, tracking problems;
- who reads the report, and what they decide with it.

## 2. Check the data first

Do the date ranges and time zones match across files? What does each metric mean in this tool? Users, sessions, reach and impressions are not the same thing. Look for missing days, duplicate rows, sampling notices and tracking changes. Note every issue, and if one changes the story, say so in the summary.

## 3. Pick what matters

Report the few metrics that connect to the goals:
- **reach**: search impressions, social reach, new visitors;
- **engagement**: clicks, engagement rate, email opens and clicks;
- **results**: sign-ups, enquiries and sales from organic channels;
- **audience growth**: followers and subscribers.

Leave out numbers that don't connect to a goal, however large they are.

## 4. Compare and explain

- Show each change as a number and a percentage, with the base. Going from 2 to 4 is +100%, but it's still only 4.
- Compare with the target, the previous month and, where seasons matter, the same month last year.
- Look for causes in the detail: top and bottom content, channels, landing pages, search terms, and the month's events.
- Keep what the data shows apart from your reading of it, and say how sure you are. Two things moving together doesn't prove one caused the other.

## 5. Write the report

```
# Organic marketing report, <month and year>

Summary: <three to five sentences: the main result against goals, the main reason, the most important next step>

| Goal | Metric | This month | Last month | Change | Target | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

What worked: <insight>, shown by <data>
What didn't: <insight>, shown by <data>
Data notes: <gaps, definition changes, tracking issues>

| Next step | Why | Owner | How we'll measure it |
|---|---|---|---|
```

## Rules

- Never invent or estimate figures the data doesn't contain. Mark gaps "to confirm".
- Keep metric definitions the same from month to month, and flag any change.
- Report bad news plainly. Don't pick only the flattering numbers.
- Use aggregated data. Keep names, email addresses and other personal data out of the report, and follow the data-protection rules that apply to the business.
- Round sensibly, and keep units and currencies visible.
