# Interview to article

Find the story in a long conversation, and tell it in the interviewee's real words.

## Before you start

You need the transcript or notes, and the purpose: a customer story, an expert piece, a staff profile or a Q&A. Ask for the length, the reader, the publication's style, and what the interviewee agreed to: what's on the record, and whether they'll review the piece before it's published. Ask for anything missing in one short message.

## Steps

1. **Read the whole transcript** and note the strongest moments: clear opinions, vivid stories, surprising facts and memorable lines.
2. **Choose the angle**: the one idea the piece is about. Suggest two or three angles if there's no obvious one.
3. **Pick the quotes that carry the story.** Use direct quotes for opinion, feeling and personality, and paraphrase plain facts.
4. **Structure the piece**: open with a strong moment or quote, give the context, tell the story, and end with what's next or a strong closing line.
5. **Keep quotes accurate.** You can cut filler words like "um", and use ellipses for cuts, but don't change the meaning or combine separate answers into one quote.
6. **Mark everything to check**: names, titles, figures, dates and claims about other people or companies.
7. **Prepare for review**, if the interviewee will check the piece: list the quotes and facts for them to confirm.

## Output

```
Angle: <one sentence>
Headline: <option>, plus two alternatives

<the article>

Quotes used: <list, with their place in the transcript>
To confirm: <names, figures, claims>
```

## Rules

- **Never invent or improve quotes.** Every quote must be in the transcript.
- Leave out anything the interviewee said was off the record, and private details about others.
- Don't put words in the interviewee's mouth through paraphrase either. Keep their meaning.
- Get consent before publishing personal details, and follow the data protection rules that apply.

## Checklist before you finish

- [ ] The angle is clear from the opening.
- [ ] Every quote matches the transcript.
- [ ] Off-the-record material is left out.
- [ ] Facts and names to check are listed.
