I wrote ten Claude skills for the people I teach.

Each one does one job, the way I'd want it done.

Here's what they do, so you can write your own versions.

What's inside:

- Two for learning
- Two for research
- Two for your career
- Two for everyday work
- One for data
- One that writes the others

::subscribe

---

## Learn and remember

### learn-anything

A patient tutor for any topic.

It asks what you already know and makes a short plan. Then it explains one idea at a time, with an analogy and a small example, and checks you understood before moving on.

```prompt Try
Teach me how neural networks learn. I know basic algebra.
```

### quiz-me

Turns your notes, a document or a topic into practice questions, asked one at a time.

It explains every answer, keeps track of what you miss and comes back to it.

```prompt Try
Quiz me on these lecture notes. Ten questions, mixed.
```

---

## Research and explain

### research-brief

Answers a question with a one-page, sourced brief.

It separates what's known from what's only claimed, gives each finding a confidence level and never invents a source.

### news-explainer

Turns AI and tech news into a plain explainer: what happened, why it matters, who it affects and what's still unknown.

Every claim is attributed. No hype.

```prompt Try
Explain this announcement for my newsletter readers.
```

---

## Grow your career

### linkedin-post

Writes LinkedIn posts in your own voice, from an idea, a lesson or a piece of work.

You get an opening worth reading, one clear point, and no invented stories or engagement bait.

```prompt Try
Turn this project I finished into a post. Here are two of my old posts.
```

### job-application

Tailors your CV and cover letter to one job ad.

It matches your real experience to what the employer asks for, and tells you honestly where the gaps are.

```prompt Try
Here's my CV and the job ad. Tailor both.
```

---

## Get work done

### meeting-to-actions

Turns meeting notes or a transcript into decisions, actions with owners and deadlines, open questions and a follow-up email.

It never invents an owner or a date.

```prompt Try
Here's the transcript of today's team call.
```

### presentation-planner

Plans a talk before you open slide software: the story, one message per slide, titles that state the point, visuals and speaker notes.

```prompt Try
Plan a ten-minute talk on our quarterly results for the board.
```

---

## Work with data

### check-the-numbers

Answers questions about spreadsheets, CSV files and SQL results, and proves the answer first.

A script profiles each file for missing values, numbers stored as text, repeated rows and gaps in the dates. Every join is checked for multiplied rows.

```prompt Try
Which products drove last quarter's revenue? Here's the export.
```

---

## Make your own

### skill-maker

Interviews you about a task you repeat, writes the SKILL.md, checks it against the rules and packages the folder as a zip, ready to upload.

```prompt Try
Help me turn the way I write our weekly report into a skill.
```

---

## Start with one

Don't write ten.

Pick the job you explain to Claude most often, and make that your first skill.
