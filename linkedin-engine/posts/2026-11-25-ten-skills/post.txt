10 Claude skills worth writing, and the exact request to try each one with today:

(No setup yet. Start in a normal chat.)

1. learn-anything
Asks what you already know, then explains one idea at a time.
→ "Teach me how neural networks learn. I know basic algebra."

2. quiz-me
Turns your notes into questions, one at a time & comes back to the ones you miss.
→ "Quiz me on these lecture notes. Ten questions, mixed."

3. research-brief
A one-page brief with real sources & a confidence level for each finding.

4. news-explainer
What happened, why it matters, who it affects, what's still unknown.
→ "Explain this announcement for my newsletter readers."

5. linkedin-post
Learns your voice from your old posts. No invented stories.
→ "Turn this project I finished into a post. Here are two of my old posts."

6. job-application
Tailors your CV to one job ad & tells you honestly where the gaps are.
→ "Here's my CV and the job ad. Tailor both."

7. meeting-to-actions
Decisions, owners, deadlines & a follow-up email. It never invents an owner.
→ "Here's the transcript of today's team call."

8. presentation-planner
One message per slide, before you open the slide app.
→ "Plan a ten-minute talk on our quarterly results for the board."

9. check-the-numbers
A script checks the file first: missing values, numbers stored as text, repeated rows.
→ "Which products drove last quarter's revenue? Here's the export."

10. skill-maker
Interviews you, then writes & zips your own skill.
→ "Help me turn the way I write our weekly report into a skill."

Now the real lesson: DON'T write ten.

Look at your last week of chats.
Find the job you explained more than once.
That's your first skill.

Three checks before you start:
☑ It's one job, not three
☑ You've explained it to Claude three times already
☑ You can say when to use it, in one line
