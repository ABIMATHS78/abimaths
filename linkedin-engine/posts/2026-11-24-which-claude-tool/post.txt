Claude gives you 4 ways to teach it how you work.

Pick the wrong one, and you'll repeat yourself forever.

Here's which one to use, and when (save this):

1. Custom instructions
→ Your general preferences.
→ Apply to every conversation.
→ Use for: your tone, your language, how long answers should be.

2. Projects
→ Background documents and knowledge.
→ Apply only to the chats inside that project.
→ Use for: one client, one course, one big piece of work.

3. Connectors
→ Access to your other apps and data, like your drive or calendar.
→ Used when Claude needs that app.
→ Use for: "find the latest version in my drive."

4. Skills
→ A way of doing one particular job.
→ Load only when a request needs it, in any chat.
→ Use for: the weekly report, the meeting summary, the email in your team's format.

My rule for skills:
Once you've explained the same thing to Claude 3 times, make it a skill.

And they work together.
A connector lets Claude reach your project tracker.
A skill teaches it how your team writes tickets.

Still not sure? Ask 2 questions:
→ Is it a way of doing one job? Explained it 3 times? Make it a skill. Not yet? Just ask in the chat.
→ Not one job? If it should apply to every chat, use instructions. If not, a project.
