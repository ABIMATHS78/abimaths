Don't open Claude again until you've read this:

(then forward it to your whole team)

> first, the one switch skills need
> settings → capabilities → "Create and edit files"
> on Team plans, skills are on by default
> on Enterprise? an owner has to turn them on first
> then know this: Claude remembers how you work in 5 places

CUSTOM INSTRUCTIONS (the note on the fridge)
> how you like things, in every single chat
> plain English, short answers, words you never want to see
> start with: "Write in plain English. Keep answers short. Ask me before you assume anything about my work."
> never paste one job's steps in here. they follow you everywhere

PROJECTS (the folder for one client)
> one space per client, course or big piece of work
> add the files once. every chat inside the project can read them
> try: "Using the files here, summarise what changed this month."
> it only works inside that project. open the project first

SKILLS (the recipe card)
> one job, done your way, in any chat
> Claude reads a one-line description to decide when to use it
> install: Customize → Skills → + → Create skill → Upload a skill
> upload the zip as it is. don't unzip it
> to be sure, name it: "Use my weekly-report skill on these notes."
> one skill, one job. not "my-work". "weekly-report"

CONNECTORS (the spare keys)
> let Claude into your other apps: your drive, your calendar
> use them when the answer lives somewhere else
> access isn't instruction. a connector reaches your files
> it doesn't teach Claude how you like the work done

JUST ASK (the quick question)
> a one-off job? no setup. explain it in the chat
> save the prompt you liked, in case it comes back
> explained it 3 times? now it's a skill

BONUS: stack them
> the connector reaches your project tracker
> the skill knows how your team writes tickets
> the project holds the background
> your instructions keep it all in plain English
