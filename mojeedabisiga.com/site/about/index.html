About Mojeed Abisiga

Abisiga Mojeed Damilola: mathematician, data scientist, founder and educator on a mission to make data and AI easy for everyone.

Mojeed Abisiga (full name Abisiga Mojeed Damilola; born 17 May 1996) is a Nigerian-born, United States-based data and artificial intelligence (AI) leader, technology entrepreneur, educator and public speaker. He is the founder and chief executive officer of AI Every Time, a 24/7 AI newsroom and AI content hub, and the co-founder and chief executive officer of DataGlobal Hub, a global media company for data science and AI news, education and events.16

Early life and education

Abisiga was born on 17 May 1996 in Surulere, Lagos, Nigeria. He traces his interest in data to an early fascination with numbers.1 He studied mathematics at the University of Lagos from 2014 to 2018, earning a Bachelor of Science degree with First Class Honours in November 2018. In April 2018 he won the gold medal at Nigeria's National Mathematics Competition for University Students (NAMCUS). That July he was one of three University of Lagos students who represented Nigeria at the 25th International Mathematics Competition for University Students (IMC) in Blagoevgrad, Bulgaria, and received a certificate.1518

As a schoolboy in Lagos he won a run of state science and mathematics competitions, including the Lagos State Climate Change Quiz Competition (2010), the Science Olympiad in chemistry (2011) and the Science Teachers Association of Nigeria quiz, which he went on to contest nationally. In October 2011 he joined a climate change research tour of Germany sponsored by Nigeria's Ministries of Education and Environment, and was one of seven students featured in a German newspaper for their impact on a UNESCO project. His education was supported by more than a dozen scholarships, among them the NNPC/Total National Merit, MTN Foundation and University of Lagos Endowment scholarships (see achievements).

After working in consulting, he moved to the United States for graduate study. On 5 May 2023 he completed a Master of Science in Informatics and Analytics at the University of North Carolina at Greensboro with a 4.00/4.00 GPA, summa cum laude, supported by a full-ride scholarship and a graduate fellowship. In 2022 he was invited into the Phi Kappa Phi honor society, which admits the top 10% of graduate students, and he delivered the keynote at his department's 2023 graduation ceremony.16

Career

Consulting and machine learning

Abisiga began his career in data science and analytics at KPMG Nigeria and Deloitte, where he led machine-learning innovation projects. As an experienced officer in KPMG's Data & Analytics team (2019–2021) he built churn-prediction and customer-segmentation models behind an 89% customer retention rate, a COVID-19 exposure analytics tool and a Twitter text-mining application that reached 10,000 active users in three months. In 2021 he was an associate in Deloitte's Enterprise Technology & Performance practice. Since June 2023 he has worked in big data engineering, where his Hadoop Resource Estimator, a machine-learning model, cut memory wastage from 60% to 22%.914 In 2019 he co-authored a research paper on applying neural architecture search to end-to-end automatic speech recognition. It was published as an arXiv preprint and has been cited 16 times.1112

DataGlobal Hub

Abisiga co-founded DataGlobal Hub with Justin Lewis and leads it as chief executive officer and editor-in-chief, setting its editorial strategy and leading a distributed team of writers and contributors.6 He has described his ambition as building "the Forbes of the data field".3 To that end, the company covers data science and AI news, free courses and workshops, job postings, events, summits, hackathons and competitions.4

DataGlobal Hub organizes the Global Data & AI Virtual Tech Conference (GDAI). Its January 2025 Book Authors' Edition drew thousands of virtual attendees from more than 60 countries and more than 150,000 views across media platforms. Speakers came from OpenAI, Microsoft, Google, Amazon and Intel.6 In November 2024 the company introduced country-specific AI news reporters.8 In May 2025 it convened the Phoenix Tech Festival on AI & Innovation at the University of Advancing Technology in Tempe, Arizona.7 DataGlobal Hub has partnered with organizations including Microsoft, Packt and DataCamp.9

AI Every Time

Abisiga is the founder and CEO of AI Every Time, a 24/7 AI newsroom and AI content hub. Users interact with AI reporters and learning companions from different countries.1 Its AI Learning Companions launched on Product Hunt in June 2026 under the tagline "The world, reported by your local AI voices that never sleep."13 He has said the purpose "is not to remove human expertise" but to make trusted understanding easier to reach.25

Other roles

He is co-founder and chief operating officer of DataRango, a gamified data and AI learning platform,6 and chief data officer of The Strategemist, where he also sits on the advisory board.914

Writing, research and speaking

Abisiga has been a member of the invitation-only Forbes Technology Council since 2025. His work there includes the bylined article "AI Conferences: Where Human Intelligence Meets Artificial Potential" (September 2025) and expert panels on AI agents, robotics, ambient IoT and digital transformation.910 He speaks on artificial intelligence, generative AI, machine learning, data strategy and leadership.14 His appearances include Venture Café Phoenix and DataGlobal Hub's GDAI conferences. As @abimaths he shares AI and data education with more than 60,000 followers across Instagram, LinkedIn and Threads, and he has completed more than 200 brand collaborations.17
