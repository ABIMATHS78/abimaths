About

Who is Mojeed Abisiga?

Mojeed Abisiga (full name Abisiga Mojeed Damilola; born 17 May 1996) is a Nigerian-born, United States-based data and artificial intelligence (AI) leader, technology entrepreneur, educator and public speaker. He is the founder and chief executive officer of AI Every Time, a 24/7 AI newsroom and AI learning platform, and of DataGlobal Hub, a global media company for data science and AI news, education and events that organizes the Global Data & AI Virtual Tech Conference (GDAI).

He graduated in mathematics from the University of Lagos with First Class Honours and was one of three students who represented Nigeria at the 25th International Mathematics Competition for University Students in Blagoevgrad, Bulgaria, in 2018. He then worked in data science and analytics at KPMG Nigeria and Deloitte, leading machine-learning innovation projects. In 2023 he earned a Master of Science in Informatics and Analytics from the University of North Carolina at Greensboro with a perfect 4.00 GPA, summa cum laude. He holds a U.S. EB-1A "Einstein visa" green card, granted for extraordinary ability, is a member of the Forbes Technology Council, and shares practical AI education with more than 60,000 followers as @abimaths.
