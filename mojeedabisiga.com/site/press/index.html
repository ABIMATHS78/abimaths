Long bio · 198 words

Mojeed Abisiga (full name Abisiga Mojeed Damilola) is an award-winning data and AI leader, technology entrepreneur, educator and public speaker on a mission to make data and AI easy for everyone. He is the founder and CEO of AI Every Time, a 24/7 AI newsroom and AI content hub where people learn from AI reporters and learning companions. He is also the co-founder and CEO of DataGlobal Hub, a global media company for data science and AI news, education and events. Through DataGlobal Hub he hosts the Global Data & AI Virtual Tech Conference (GDAI), which has featured speakers from OpenAI, Microsoft, Google, Amazon and Intel and drawn attendees from more than 60 countries. As a schoolboy he won Lagos State's Climate Change Quiz Competition and a research tour of Germany. He studied mathematics at the University of Lagos, competed at the International Mathematics Competition for University Students, led machine-learning projects at KPMG Nigeria and Deloitte, and earned an M.S. in Informatics and Analytics from UNC Greensboro with a perfect 4.0 GPA, summa cum laude. He is a U.S. EB-1A "Einstein visa" recipient, a LinkedIn Top Voice in Machine Learning and a member of the Forbes Technology Council.
