Professional impact

  • Big data engineering (2023–present): built the Hadoop Resource Estimator, a machine-learning model that recommends memory for Hadoop jobs and cut memory wastage from 60% to 22%; helped refine the RAISE model, contributing to a 50–60% reduction in execution times; automated database clean-up, reducing server load by 25%.
  • KPMG, Data & Analytics (2019–2021): churn-prediction and customer-segmentation models behind an 89% customer retention rate; an applicant tracking system that cut hiring time by 40%; migration of firm data from a local to a global server to the required IBS standard using SQL; a COVID-19 exposure analytics tool that contributed to a 30% fall in cases; a real-time Twitter text-mining web app that reached 10,000 active users in its first three months; automated verification of quarterly financial reports, halving manual effort.
  • Deloitte, Enterprise Technology & Performance (2021): designed the survey questionnaires and interview guides (20+ targeted questions and prompts) for a digital maturity assessment of two tier-1 clients and interviewed six of their senior representatives.
  • UNC Greensboro (2021–2023): research and office assistant in statistics and machine learning, supporting lecturers with grading and student office hours, and social media campaigns that grew the Informatics & Analytics programme's online presence.
  • DataGlobal Hub (2023–present): as co-founder and editor-in-chief, built a global AI and data media platform; commissions and publishes articles from industry leaders, founders and researchers; leads a distributed team of writers and contributors; grew its audience through newsletters, SEO and strategic partnerships; developed articles, video interviews, podcasts and infographics; positioned it as an ecosystem connecting media, founders, investors and AI practitioners; and produced and moderated large virtual conferences with global industry leaders.
  • Earlier: ETL pipelines and an executive dashboard as a data intern at SEAMFIX (2019); Twitter sentiment analysis and KPIs for the publicity team of an Africa Freestyle Football Competition (2019).
